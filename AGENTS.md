# AGENTS.md

## Project
This project is a wedding livestream landing page for a couple with a soft, celebratory New Year’s / evening aesthetic.

## Core design rules
- Use a soft black or near-black background with warm gold accents.
- Keep typography elegant and high-contrast, using gold or ivory tones on dark backgrounds.
- Maintain a celebratory, romantic, NYE-inspired mood without making the page feel overly loud or cluttered.
- Prefer a refined, minimal layout with generous spacing.

## Photo and layout rules
- Use the couple’s landscape photo as a feature image, but do not let text sit on top of the people’s faces.
- On desktop, a hero banner with a subtle dark overlay is acceptable.
- On mobile, never overlap text and faces. Use a stacked layout: photo first, then content below.
- Keep the photo faded or darkened as needed to preserve readability, but do not obscure the subjects in a way that feels awkward.

## Mobile-first responsiveness
- Always prioritize mobile readability and usability.
- Ensure no text, cards, or buttons overflow or cover important parts of the photo on smaller screens.
- Use responsive layouts that stack cleanly on phones.
- Keep text blocks short, readable, and easy to scan.
- Maintain comfortable spacing and readable contrast across breakpoints.

## Content guidance
- Make it clear the page is for a wedding or wedding livestream, especially for visitors who land on it unexpectedly.
- Keep event details concise: names, date, time, and livestream call-to-action.
- If using a livestream button, make it obvious and easy to tap on mobile.

## Quality bar
- Preserve the premium black-and-gold aesthetic.
- Keep the layout elegant and intentional.
- Favor readability over decorative complexity.
- If a design choice looks good on desktop but fails on mobile, prioritize the mobile version.
